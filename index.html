An executive assistant that actually knows how you work.

CEO Sidekick pulls your email, calendar, files and team messages into one place — then helps you act on them. It remembers your priorities, your people and your working style, so you stop re-explaining yourself.

Today

Your to-dos and appointments in one view, assembled from every connected account.

Assistant

Ask for a draft, a summary, or a meeting time. It reads the thread and acts in context.

Voice notes

Capture a thought while driving. It's transcribed, filed, and turned into tasks.

Memory

What you tell it once, it keeps. You can review and delete anything it has learned.

Connect the tools you already use

Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive), Microsoft (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive), Slack and Notion. Connect several accounts at once — work and personal, or two companies side by side — and switch between them without signing out.

You authorize each account through that provider's own sign-in screen. CEO Sidekick never asks for, sees, or stores your passwords.

Built to be private

Your data is used to run the features you asked for — nothing else. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. You can disconnect an account or delete your data at any time from inside the app.

The details are in the privacy policy, including exactly what each connected service gives us access to and why.
